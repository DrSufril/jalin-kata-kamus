# Jalin Kata Support

Jalin Kata is a Bahasa Malaysia word-board game for iPhone and iPad. Play against the computer or take turns with a friend on one device.

## How to play

Tap a tile in your rack, then an empty square. Your first word must cover the center square. Later words must connect to tiles already on the board. Tap **Hantar** (Submit) to confirm your move, the lightbulb for a hint, **Tukar jubin** to exchange tiles, or **Lalu** to pass.

If you think a rejected word should be accepted, tap **Kata ini sepatutnya sah?**. The app saves the report on your device. Open **Kata dilaporkan** on the main menu to view or share it.
