# Jalin Kata Privacy Policy

This policy describes how Jalin Kata for iPhone and iPad handles data.

## Data collection

Jalin Kata does not require an account. The developer does not collect personal data through the app. The game uses a bundled dictionary and runs its computer opponent and local two-player mode on the device.

## Data stored on your device

The app stores preferences and local gameplay information on your device. If you flag a rejected word, the app stores the report on your device. It does not send reports automatically. If you choose Share, iOS or iPadOS lets you decide where to send the report.

## Contacting support

If you contact the developer, the developer can read the information you choose to send and uses it to respond. GitHub issues are public and are governed by GitHub’s privacy policies. Do not include personal information in an issue.

See [Jalin Kata Support](support.md) for the contact link.
